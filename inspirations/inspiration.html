Here are the Video games I played during my Childhood

Crash Twinsanity

I played this game on my Ps2 as it was part of the Crash Action Pack I got as a kid, and I remeber playing it the most due to being the only one that is not a Kart Racers which are Crash Nitro Kart and Crash Tag Team Racing.
It was a charming and quirky game with cutscenes that make me laugh sometimes, open world platforming that felt fun to explore despite the difficulty in getting the gems and camera issues.
And lastly, it come at a time where it was the worst selling Crash game on console, so I was one of the few lucky one that got it.

Tekken 5

I play this game back in the late 2000s where it one of the Ps2 games I owned and one of the two fightings games I played with the other being Mortal Kombat Armageddon. Despite being a fighting game, I never got around to the fighting part, just the modes like the 3d beat em ups, arcade history, and I believe to be practice as this is my first fighting game, as I have a lot to learn if I want to enjoy it. And due to me never finding out about the second version at the time, I never experience the other modes like the Tekken Bowl, Tekken Dojo, and Gold Rush so what I got was the 1.0 version.

Wario Land 4

Wario Land 4 is one of the few games I play on the Game Boy Advance that isn't a remix of a classic title like Super Mario World and Super Mario Bros 2 and 3 which made me feel what a challenging game is like.
When I first play it, it was fast paces and engaging with a well-explained tutorial level that introduces the moveset (dash, ground pound, and enemy throwing).
And after beating the first boss whether it easy or hard depends of what items you used, it let me choose which passages I can proceed.
What kept me going is the visual style where it feature some of the most details and expression animation sprite work at the time in a way that push the Game Boy Advance to its limits.
And also that instead of an end of the level goal like most platformers, it has what people called a heist loop where it built around a two-phase structure.
