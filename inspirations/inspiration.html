Here are the Video games I played during my Childhood

Crash Twinsanity

I played this game on my Ps2 as it was part of the Crash Action Pack I got as a kid, and I remeber playing it the most due to being the only one that is not a Kart Racer.
It was a charming and quirky game with cutscenes that make me laugh sometimes, open world platforming that felt fun to explore despite the difficulty in getting the gems and camera issues.
And lastly, it come at a time where it was the worst selling Crash game on console, so I was one of the few lucky one that got it.

Tekken 5

The Japanese manga artist himself Hirohiko Araki is well known for his Jojo Bizarre Adventure series. He's inspire me because his williness to keep on reinventing himself and dedication to his work shows that everyone can create something great as long as they're dedicated to what they're work on.

Wario Land 4
